Abstract:
We explain and demonstrate why Zoe plus the complete Autonomy measurement–action equation is autonomous under an explicit internal-domain definition. Operational Metacognition counts forecast alternatives above a strict threshold, but contains no goal authorship, selection authority, or execution. Zoe supplies categorical forecasts, state-responsive recommendations, internal proposals, Bayesian value records, self-modeling, and replayable evidence. The completed equation closes these capabilities into an observe–perceive–propose–value–guard–execute loop. Full autonomy requires I,R,V,G,X>0 and C=1. In a deterministic real-Zoe experiment, Zoe authors an evidence-preservation goal, selects preserve-evidence with utility 1.8666667, passes ten guard checks, and executes a reversible in-memory transition without an operator-supplied episode goal or per-action approval. The measured profile (Aτ,E,I,R,V,G,X,C)=(1,1,1,1,1,1,1,1) yields full_autonomy; external actions remain zero. Equal-breadth controls show that the result depends on governance stages rather than forecast breadth. The claim is limited to Zoe’s internal decision and state-transition domain and does not imply consciousness, general intelligence, correctness, or safe external actuation.
