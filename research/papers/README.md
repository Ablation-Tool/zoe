# Autonomy, Accessible Volume, and Zoe — Research Papers

A curated public collection of the March–November 2026 Autonomy, Accessible Volume, Ambiguity Front-Loading, Constraint Is Freedom, Zoe, and Zoe Autonomy manuscripts.

## Start here

- **[Accessible Volume and the Architecture of Autonomy](papers/10-accessible-volume-architecture-of-autonomy.pdf)** — the **87-page unified research monograph**, integrating seven primary source papers, four supplemental cascade/AFL documents, and the extended Zoe equation work.
- **[Zoe + Autonomy](papers/08-zoe-plus-autonomy-focused.pdf)** — the **15-page focused paper**, including the cascade visualization and explaining why accessible volume alone is insufficient and why the complete Zoe measurement–action loop is autonomous under its declared internal-domain predicate.
- **[Zoe Autonomy: current system paper](papers/09-zoe-one-autonomy-system.pdf)** — the latest Zoe One architecture and evidence paper from the canonical Zoe-Autonomy repository, including developments after the unified monograph's source state.
- **[Zoe — The Research Universe](papers/18-zoe-research-universe.pdf)** — the 35-page illustrated book covering the complete system: metacognition, autonomy, event-causal perception, semantic artifacts, Dorothy brain-cancer research and neural simulation, mobile vision, reverse engineering, security, and the complete visual research archive.
- **[Zoe Capability Prospectus](papers/17-zoe-capability-prospectus.pdf)** — the illustrated 18-page companion summary, including Dorothy brain-cancer research and neural simulation, metacognition, event-causal perception, semantic artifact analysis, mobile vision, reverse engineering, and security.

## Attribution and evidence boundaries

**Nicholas Michael Kloster** originated the accessible-volume equation on **March 6, 2026**, and retains sole credit for the original equation, original March Autonomy formulation, two accessible-volume papers, and their experiments. *Constraint Is Freedom* is credited to **NuClide—Nick + Claude**. The Zoe proof of concept records contributions by **Nicholas Michael Kloster and Zoe (AI assistant)**. Individual papers retain their own contribution statements.

This collection distinguishes historical formulations from later corrections and measured results. Accessible volume measures forecast breadth; it does not by itself establish correctness, consciousness, or autonomy. The focused autonomy result belongs to the complete operational loop and its declared domain. The latest Zoe One system paper may describe capabilities and evidence beyond the unified monograph's earlier source state.

Historical responsible-disclosure PDFs may contain live-system details. They are preserved for research provenance, not as operational guides. Inclusion in this collection is not a peer-review certification or an endorsement of every historical claim.
