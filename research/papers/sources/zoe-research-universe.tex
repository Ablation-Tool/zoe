\section{Why Zoe exists}

Zoe is not one model, one prompt, or one benchmark. It is an inspectable native workbench and source distribution: \textbf{16 registered engines across 12 source projects}, joined by one canonical autonomy engine and a shared evidence discipline.

\card{THE RESEARCH COLLABORATOR MISSION}{Give Zoe the ability to perceive, think, learn, and act in a closed loop; to observe her own uncertainty; to build and revise models; to inspect code and artifacts; and to help with the research that matters—from brain cancer to reverse engineering, from CERN to the next token.}

The destination is a research collaborator that can do more than answer. It can look at a problem, choose what to inspect, measure what happened, and decide what to do next—while showing the provenance of every choice.

\section{We built metacognition}

\begin{center}
\Large\color{ZoeCyan!60!black}Zoe can watch how many realistic choices remain open before she chooses.\par
\end{center}

For a next-token distribution $p$ and threshold $\tau$, Accessible Volume is
\begin{equation}
A_\tau(p)=\left|\{x:p(x)>\tau\}\right|.
\end{equation}

If someone else only measures $A_\tau$, it is a measurement. When Zoe computes or receives that count from her own decision distribution and uses it to assess uncertainty and guide later inspection, she is observing her own decision process: \textbf{operational metacognition}.

\card{NOT JUST A NUMBER}{Accessible Volume lets Zoe see how open or settled a decision is. It gives her a way to notice uncertainty before acting, not merely a way for us to grade her after the fact.}

The full operational profile adds effective perception, internal proposal generation, state responsiveness, value-based selection, internal goal authorship, internally authorized execution, and guard compliance:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal A=(A_\tau,E,I,R,V,G,X,C),\qquad I,R,V,G,X>0,\quad C=1.
\end{equation}

This is real, bounded metacognition. It is not consciousness, emotion, or human-like self-awareness. It is a concrete ability to inspect and use one's own decision state.

\evidence{\url{https://github.com/Ablation-Tool/next-token-predictability} and \url{https://github.com/Ablation-Tool/Zoe-Autonomy/tree/main/evidence/self-reflection}}

\section{The loop is the invention}

Zoe's event-causal architecture links five capabilities that are often demonstrated separately and called a system.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Perceive:} sparse polarity events reconstruct an occupancy scene. Spatial coherence and whole-scene translation estimate what changed, rather than simply where pixels changed.
  \item \textbf{Imagine:} a compositional forward ensemble predicts candidate event-stream outcomes. Exact nonnegative Dijkstra search evaluates attention trajectories.
  \item \textbf{Remember:} key-value episodic records connect context, action, and observed outcome with previous-record hashes.
  \item \textbf{Doubt:} independent uncertainty combines support distance, ensemble disagreement, and polarity anomaly. It can force inspection or abstention.
  \item \textbf{Learn and act:} gated local updates and interleaved replay change later attention. Failed consolidation restores the checkpoint.
\end{enumerate}

\card{CAUSAL PERCEPTION}{Two different event scenes can now produce different internally selected attention actions. Vision is no longer a saliency image attached to a constant preserve-evidence decision.}

\section{She sees change}

Zoe accepts sparse events $e_i=(t_i,x_i,y_i,p_i)$, with $p_i\in\{-1,+1\}$, from synthetic fixtures, DVS-style event records, or RGB frame differences.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Four sparse spatial modules integrate local evidence.
  \item Polarity updates reconstruct the current scene instead of treating new and vacated pixels as an object center.
  \item Spatially coherent components suppress isolated noise.
  \item Whole-scene translation matching follows the best-supported motion of the shape.
  \item Leaky integration, spike-order features, and bounded STDP add temporal structure without a biological-equivalence claim.
  \item DVS-style events and camera frames use the same shared representation and evidence chain.
\end{itemize}

\card{REAL INPUT}{The physical camera path produced valid sparse change events and a guarded decision. No audio was captured and no photograph was retained.}

\subsection{Computed visual attention}

The canonical iLab-inspired saliency engine computes nine Gaussian scales and 42 intensity, color-opponency, and orientation maps. Winner selection and inhibition of return produce an attention scanpath; exact Dijkstra connects selected foci over the unchanged pre-inhibition field.

\evidence{\url{https://github.com/Ablation-Tool/graphics-environment-manager/blob/main/COGNITION.md\#multiscale-rgb-saliency-and-attention}}
\boundary{Saliency is computed local conspicuity, not semantic object recognition, human fixation, or subjective sight.}

\clearpage
\section{She imagines consequences}

Before acting, Zoe's forward ensemble evaluates what each candidate is expected to observe next. Position, direction, and appearance are modeled separately so learned dynamics can be recomposed rather than memorized as one opaque state.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Candidate predictions include a target distribution, expected centroid, visibility, variance, and retrieved-episode count.
  \item Exact nonnegative Dijkstra evaluates a time-expanded attention graph.
  \item Utility includes goal value, expected information gain, and effort.
  \item Internal goals change between tracking, localization, and inspection/abstention according to measured state.
  \item Reversible attention changes the next observation window and therefore later perception.
\end{itemize}

\card{PREDICTION BEFORE ACTION}{Every non-abstain candidate supplies a predicted next observation and uncertainty before Zoe chooses. The guard is not a post-hoc explanation; it participates in the choice.}

\subsection{Independent uncertainty}

\begin{equation}
U_t=.55D_t+.35V_t+.10A_t,
\end{equation}
where $D_t$ is support distance, $V_t$ is ensemble disagreement, and $A_t$ is polarity anomaly. An intervention suppresses confident updates and selects abstention independently of candidate utility.

\boundary{Uncertainty is an engineering proxy for epistemic state, not emotional confidence or a guarantee against error.}

\clearpage
\section{She remembers}

Zoe's memory is not one opaque context window. It separates perceptual, action, and uncertainty working state; immutable event episodes; forward-model parameters; and source provenance.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Working memory:} independent perception, action, and uncertainty subspaces.
  \item \textbf{Episodic memory:} causal transition records with keys, values, and previous-record hashes.
  \item \textbf{Retrieval:} sparse normalized weights over key similarity; retrieval informs but never replaces current observation.
  \item \textbf{Replay:} interleaved new and familiar episodes with validation and reversible checkpoints.
  \item \textbf{Persistent memory:} SQLite sources, claims, lifecycle state, contradictions, content hashes, and export.
  \item \textbf{Analysis memory:} Ablation's architecture-specific knowledge graph and confirmed-finding history.
\end{itemize}

\card{MEMORY WITH PROVENANCE}{A memory is not just content. It carries where it came from, which action produced it, what uncertainty attended it, and whether it can be replayed without corrupting the chain.}

\subsection{Soroban working memory}

Earth and heaven beads encode exact integer state, carries, borrows, and reversible operations. Observation and adoption counters use real bead transitions rather than integer decode shortcuts. The counters remain separate from likelihood weights.

\evidence{\url{https://github.com/Ablation-Tool/graphics-environment-manager/blob/main/COGNITION.md\#soroban-working-memory-and-ecological-feedback}}

\section{She learns without erasing}

Local plasticity is gated rather than allowed to rewrite the whole system on every observation. Instructive evidence assigns credit; bounded plastic state stores the update. Abstention suppresses the write.

\begin{equation}
\Delta w_t=g_t\eta\delta_t\phi_t,\qquad w_{t+1}=\operatorname{clip}(w_t+\Delta w_t,w_{\min},w_{\max}).
\end{equation}

Consolidation interleaves novel and familiar episodes and is adopted only when recent validation improves and familiar retention does not regress beyond the declared tolerance. A failed validation restores the prior checkpoint.

\card{SELF-CORRECTION}{A system that can say ``this made me worse'' and restore its prior model is more useful than a system that merely keeps changing.}

\boundary{Continual adaptation is demonstrated on declared models and replay fixtures. It is not a general solution to catastrophic forgetting or proof of new-skill acquisition across domains.}

\clearpage
\section{She chooses—and can be stopped}

Internally authored goals and deterministic value ranking choose among declared actions. The guard checks goal origin, proposal origin, reversibility, allowlists, and any enabled external scope.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Internal attention and in-memory transitions execute without per-step operator selection.
  \item Host, network, and hardware operations require a separate owner grant.
  \item The emergency stop is checked before external operations, including candidates already selected by policy.
  \item External operations never elevate privileges or transfer to another computer.
  \item Unknown, irreversible, externally authored, and ungranted actions are rejected.
\end{itemize}

\card{AUTONOMY WITH A STOP CONDITION}{Zoe can choose without being asked at every step, but the owner can still stop the external path. Capability and control are designed together.}

\section{She reads the machine}

Ablation gives Zoe a second kind of semantics: the meaning of executable artifacts. It does not pretend that reverse engineering is natural-language understanding. It supplies structure, behavior, call relationships, vulnerability evidence, and analysis history.

\begin{itemize}

  \item Normalized opcode categories turn ISA-specific instructions into behavioral descriptions.
  \item BGE embeddings rank candidates by semantic similarity.
  \item Capstone independently checks direct calls and tail jumps in \texttt{.text}.
  \item Zoe's Cognitive Field stores concepts; the guard decides what to inspect next.
  \item Ablation's knowledge graph supplies architecture-specific tools, gaps, and prior sessions.

\section{Dorothy's brain in code}

The Dorothy system also includes a neural-signal and generative research world. It is not a whole-brain or patient simulation. It is a set of inspectable teaching and simulation mechanisms with explicit boundaries.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Neural signals:} DFT band powers, delay embedding, Lyapunov estimation, Higuchi dimension, and Shannon entropy on synthetic data.
  \item \textbf{Spatial models:} synthetic volumes, separable Gaussian point-spread convolution, projections, pooling, and recovery measurements.

\section{Mobile vision and accessibility}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Guided Vision:} explicit camera start and one-frame inspection, bundled offline OCR and image labels, optional device AI only when explicitly enabled, and spoken results on request.
  \item \textbf{Multi-view:} independent phone back, phone front, and optional laptop feeds with separate status and failure paths. Concurrent-camera capability is checked by binding real cameras rather than inferred from feature flags.
  \item \textbf{Trinity:} Tiny/mobile/laptop measurement planes, strict floating-point checks, local-only journal options, and no camera or microphone use by the Trinity experiment itself.
  \item \textbf{Field kit:} Acoustic ranging, radio field, location field, connectivity, screen space, app observatory, SafeConvert, and researcher guide.
  \item \textbf{Local bridge:} laptop MJPEG over \texttt{127.0.0.1} with ffmpeg and optional ADB reverse; no open LAN camera server, cloud relay, or trust-all TLS.
  \item \textbf{Apple:} explicit private-network pairing and native camera lifecycle controls; returning home explicitly unbinds the camera.
\end{itemize}

\section{Security, privacy, and evidence engineering}

\begin{itemize}

\section{Why this can become a research collaborator}

A research collaborator needs more than a large answer model. It needs a repeatable way to turn uncertainty into useful action.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Notice:} detect change, anomaly, and accessible alternatives.
  \item \textbf{Inspect:} choose the most informative observation or artifact rather than guessing.
  \item \textbf{Model:} retain a predictive account of what the world or artifact is doing.
  \item \textbf{Simulate:} compare candidate futures without rewriting the model at every step.
  \item \textbf{Check:} expose uncertainty and seek disconfirming evidence.
  \item \textbf{Act reversibly:} make a bounded transition and preserve what happened.
  \item \textbf{Learn:} update only when held-out evidence supports the change, and retain a rollback.
  \item \textbf{Explain:} return the goal, candidates, values, guard decision, outcome, and source provenance.
\end{enumerate}

\card{THE COLLABORATION}{The human supplies purpose, judgment, values, and responsibility. Zoe supplies variation, measurement, memory, and inspectable computation. The collaboration is stronger than either side pretending to be the other.}

  \item Reversible model adoption and interleaved replay rather than unchecked self-modification.

\end{itemize}
