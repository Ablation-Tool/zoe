# CERN research in Zoe

Zoe's CERN program connects read-only source evidence, deterministic retrieval, bounded replay, protocol models, virtual laboratories, and explicit process ownership. It does not claim live detector access, hardware health, analog timing closure, or deployed-system acceptance.

## Evidence inventory

The Evidence Navigator preserves C1 foundational 40, C2 subsystem 49, C3 integrated 125, and C4 cross-domain 12 previews. Full verification covers 226 source hashes, 226 preview hashes, 226 compatibility links, and 17 bridge-document links. Previews are navigation evidence, not 226 complete explanations or measurements.

The CERN handover records 9,968 inventory entries, HELIOS, Atelier, FELIX groups, LCB and STAR/Flink logical models, virtual systems, and input/vector replay. The separate Neurogarden universe preserves its own catalogue state and broken-link findings rather than hiding them.

## Seven Evidence Navigator bridges

1. Timing-aware synthetic readout replay.
2. Protocol observation ladder without a universal translator claim.
3. Guarded observe-only inspection.
4. Scene and deterministic audio sonification.
5. Shadow retrieval of saved findings without an LLM.
6. Cross-experiment design review without proposing physical connections.
7. C1–C4 complexity-directed practice without rating the reader.
