# Dorothy and Neurogarden

**Research priority:** inspectable spatial analysis and source-linked scientific review supporting a long-term brain-cancer research mission. Dorothy is not a cure, diagnostic system, treatment planner, patient registry, or surgical authority.

## Atlas geometry

Dorothy renders the Allen Mouse CCFv3 2017 parcellation: 12 selected structures, 158,326 vertices, and 315,932 triangles in the default view. Meshes and the structure graph are SHA-256-verified on every load. Rings uses exact CCF subtree volumes; Jenga supplies volume-scaled exploded and pull-out layouts; Stack cuts real meshes into coronal slabs.

| View | Research purpose | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| 3D atlas | Deterministic orbit-camera scene, clipping, shading, and export | Mouse reference geometry, not patient anatomy |
| Rings | Ontology navigation using exact subtree volumes | Volume is not functional importance or connectivity |
| Jenga | Volume-scaled spatial layers and exploded inspection | Layout is illustrative geometry |
| Stack | Coronal mesh slabs for interior inspection | Not patient registration or surgical planning |
| Neurogarden spatial bench | Synthetic PSF convolution, projections, pooling, and recovery | No scanner calibration or clinical image inference |

## Neurogarden review state

At the preserved November 14 v62 state: **37 populated books, 1,751 indexed sections, 130 reviewed entries, and 1,621 pending entries**. Review requires source hashes, concepts, applications, limitations, and a demonstrated bounded use or explicit no-tool rationale. Extraction is not comprehension. The evidence preserves bounded snippets rather than third-party book text.

The program includes Needleman–Wunsch alignment, imaging sampling and Gaussian MTF, synthetic spatial projection, SPD-matrix teaching geometry, a bounded Pennes conduction/perfusion model, EEG teaching features, neutrosophic similarity, exact integer arithmetic, and fixed synthetic radiobiology examples. None supplies diagnosis, treatment, clinical licensure, or autonomous patient action.

Negative and contradictory source findings remain visible: missing patient-level splits, incorrect specificity denominators, inconsistent MRI counts, swapped retinal plots, incomplete optimizer definitions, missing raw predictions, and unverified secondary therapeutic claims. Bounded arithmetic audits do not evaluate the original models.

## Neural simulation and generative research world

Dorothy also includes executable teaching and simulation mechanisms, not just atlas geometry and source reviews. DFT band powers, delay embedding, Lyapunov estimation, Higuchi dimension, and Shannon entropy operate on synthetic neural signals. The bounded generative world holds review-required experiments across biology, neuroscience, physics, CERN, GEM, and community research, with measurement and human review kept separate from clinical action. Additional models include synthetic point-spread convolution and recovery, bounded SPD matrix geometry, radial Pennes conduction/perfusion, neutrosophic membership similarity, exact integer arithmetic, and fixed synthetic radiobiology examples. These mechanisms do not form a whole brain, patient simulation, disease classifier, treatment plan, or clinical authority.

## Memorial and source credit

Zoe honors Dorothy McEwen Kildall, who died of brain cancer on January 31, 2005. The atlas name is a human memorial, not a source of patient anatomy. Allen Institute data are associated with Wang et al., *The Allen Mouse Brain Common Coordinate Framework: A 3D Reference Atlas*, Cell (2020), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2020.04.007.
